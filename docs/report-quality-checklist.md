# Company Intelligence Brief — quality checklist

## Entity

- [ ] Legal name, Chinese name, identifier and jurisdiction refer to the same entity.
- [ ] Brand, website, payment and contracting entities are distinguished where relevant.
- [ ] Same-name company risk is addressed.

## Sources

- [ ] Every material factual statement has a recorded source.
- [ ] Retrieval dates and source dates are visible.
- [ ] Use rights and contractual restrictions have been checked.
- [ ] Conflicting sources are identified rather than silently resolved.
- [ ] Missing information is not described as a clean result.

## Analysis

- [ ] Facts, signals and interpretation are clearly separated.
- [ ] No unsupported causal or intent claim appears.
- [ ] No statement declares the target legally compliant, safe, creditworthy, investment-grade or approved for KYC.
- [ ] Litigation and regulatory events are attributed to the correct entity.
- [ ] Material limitations are specific to the report.

## AI and language

- [ ] AI-generated text has been reviewed by a person.
- [ ] Chinese names, dates, amounts and official titles match the source.
- [ ] Translation choices preserve the legal entity type and do not invent an official English name.
- [ ] Citations resolve to the retained evidence.

## Delivery

- [ ] Report matches the confirmed scope.
- [ ] Report date and information cut-off date appear on the cover.
- [ ] Service boundary and permitted-use notice are included.
- [ ] Filename and email identify the correct customer and target.
- [ ] Final PDF opens correctly and contains no internal notes or metadata that should be removed.
